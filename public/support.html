Common questions

Why isn't the add-in appearing in PowerPoint?

Make sure you've installed SnapBack from AppSource and restarted PowerPoint. It appears in the Home tab under the Formatting group.

The title moved to the wrong position on my slide.

SnapBack reads position from your slide master. If the title is intentionally placed in a non-standard position (e.g. a section divider slide), SnapBack's 15% guard should skip it. If it moved unexpectedly, email us with the presentation attached.

The font or colour didn't change to match my master.

SnapBack reads font and colour settings from the slide master XML. Some complex masters store these in non-standard locations. Email us with your .pptx file and we'll investigate.

Can I use SnapBack on Mac and Windows?

Yes — SnapBack works on PowerPoint for Windows and Mac (Microsoft 365 subscription required).
