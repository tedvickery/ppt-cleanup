How to install
01

Open PowerPoint

Open any PowerPoint presentation on Desktop or in the browser.

02

Go to Insert → Get Add-ins

Click the Insert tab in the ribbon, then click Get Add-ins to open the Office Add-ins store.

03

Search for SnapBack

Type SnapBack in the search box and hit enter. Click Add to install.

04

Find it in the ribbon

SnapBack appears in your Home tab. Click to open the panel — it reads your master template automatically.

05

For teams

Ask your IT admin to deploy via the Microsoft 365 Admin Centre. SnapBack appears for everyone with no individual install needed.
